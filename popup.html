Community list (optional)

YouTube's own AI label is only on a small share of AI videos. You can also hide channels listed in AiSList, a community-maintained list (CC BY-NC 4.0, about 29,000 channels, with its own false-positive review process).

Turning it on downloads the list from raw.githubusercontent.com once a day; Chrome will ask for that permission. Nothing about you is sent.
