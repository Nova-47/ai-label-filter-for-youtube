Community list (optional)

YouTube labels only a small share of AI videos. You can also filter channels in AiSList, a community-maintained list (CC BY-NC 4.0, 30,000+ channels) with a false-positive review process.

When enabled, the list downloads from raw.githubusercontent.com once a day. Chrome will ask for permission. No data about you is sent.
